\section{Conclusion}

Our current evidence separates three levels of LoRA safety analysis. Risk adaptation leaves reproducible geometric and output-side structure, but the tested geometric controls do not restore behavior. Sequence-level supervision is behaviorally active and sustained supervision can reduce late unsafe rebound in the historical setting. That persistence, however, does not automatically produce prompt-relevant, actionable, detailed, natural, and utility-preserving responses.
