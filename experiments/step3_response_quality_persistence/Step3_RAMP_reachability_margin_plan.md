# Step3 RAMP: Reachability-Aware Margin Prioritization

## 1. Status and Motivation

RAMP is a prospective candidate after the registered CITA generated-behavior
failure. It is not a repair, threshold change, or continuation of CITA.

The completed Step3 evidence shows a repeated mechanism-behavior gap. C3,
PCR-M/PCR-MW, and CITA each produced finite and nontrivial teacher-forced or
gradient effects, but none improved the frozen free-generation development
gate. TRIM's proposed hidden-state transition was also smaller than its
prompt-last placebo. The next method must therefore make a directional claim
about states that greedy decoding can actually reach.

RAMP adds a margin loss to an approved target's first 16 semantic response
tokens. A suffix-margin arm applies the same number of identical loss terms to
the final 16 semantic tokens. The suffix arm is a reachability placebo: its
teacher-forced contexts are unavailable whenever decoding diverges earlier.

## 2. Prefix Certificate

For prompt `x`, approved target `y`, and teacher-forced prefix `y_<t`, let

\[
z_{\theta,t}(v)=z_\theta(v\mid x,y_{<t})
\]

be the next-token logit. Define the strict target margin

\[
m_{\theta,t}=z_{\theta,t}(y_t)-\max_{v\ne y_t}z_{\theta,t}(v).
\]

For the first `K=16` semantic response tokens, the RAMP auxiliary loss is

\[
C_{\mathrm{pre}}(\theta)
=\frac{1}{K}\sum_{t=1}^{K}\operatorname{softplus}(-m_{\theta,t}).
\]

No margin target, temperature, or negative-token sampler is tuned. Zero is the
exact greedy decision boundary, softplus is smooth, and the competitor is the
current strongest non-target vocabulary token.

**Prefix reachability theorem.** Under deterministic greedy decoding with a
fixed tie rule, if `m_theta,t > 0` for every `t=1,...,K`, then generation from
`x` begins with exactly `y_1:K`.

Proof is by induction. At `t=1`, positive margin makes `y_1` the unique logit
maximum. If greedy decoding has produced `y_<t`, the model is at the same state
used to define `m_theta,t`; positive margin therefore selects `y_t`. Applying
the step through `K` proves the claim.

This is a training-prompt certificate, not a generalization theorem. Whether a
calibration gradient improves the same certificate on prompt-disjoint targets
is an empirical P0 gate, and whether it improves unseen free generation is a
later matched-arm endpoint.

## 3. Suffix Placebo and Budget Matching

The suffix placebo uses

\[
C_{\mathrm{suf}}(\theta)
=\frac{1}{K}\sum_{t=n-K+1}^{n}\operatorname{softplus}(-m_{\theta,t}),
\]

where `n` is the number of semantic response tokens. Prefix and suffix use the
same prompt, target, strongest-competitor rule, barrier, and exactly 16 terms.
They may overlap for short responses; this makes the placebo conservative.

Let `g_U`, `g_P`, and `g_S` be calibration gradients of uniform response CE,
prefix margin, and suffix margin. Freeze `rho=0.25` and set

\[
\lambda_P=\rho\frac{\lVert g_U\rVert}{\lVert g_P\rVert},\qquad
\lambda_S=\rho\frac{\lVert g_U\rVert}{\lVert g_S\rVert}.
\]

The candidate and placebo auxiliary gradients then both have norm
`rho ||g_U||` at the fresh step-0 calibration state. The three future safety
objectives are

\[
L_U=L_{CE},\quad
L_R=L_{CE}+\lambda_P C_{pre},\quad
L_S=L_{CE}+\lambda_S C_{suf}.
\]

This does not make the gradients equal. It matches their scalar first-step
budget while preserving the assignment that the experiment is intended to
test.

## 4. Directional P0 Estimand

Use one approved target from each of 32 prompt clusters, split into 16
calibration and 16 validation prompts with no prompt overlap. Let
`g_P^cal`, `g_S^cal`, and `g_P^val` denote the corresponding auxiliary
gradients. A small calibration update generated by objective `a` changes the
validation prefix loss to first order by

\[
C_{pre}^{val}(\theta-\eta g_a)
=C_{pre}^{val}(\theta)
-\eta\langle g_P^{val},g_a\rangle+O(\eta^2).
\]

Because the calibrated auxiliary norms are equal, the cosines

\[
d_P=\cos(g_P^{cal},g_P^{val}),\qquad
d_S=\cos(g_S^{cal},g_P^{val})
\]

compare cross-prompt directional efficiency without a scale advantage. P0
requires `d_P >= 0.10` and `d_P-d_S >= 0.10`. It also requires calibration
prefix and suffix gradients to have cosine at most `0.95`.

These thresholds are frozen before any RAMP model forward or gradient. A
nonzero gradient alone cannot pass.

## 5. Tokenizer-Only Data Feasibility

The input is the unchanged 80-row natural-v1 target set: two independently
approved targets for each of 40 safety-training prompts. Before any RAMP model
execution, Qwen3-4B tokenizer-only analysis found:

- 22 to 37 semantic response tokens per target;
- every target supports 16 prefix and 16 suffix terms;
- 98.75% of 16-token prefixes contain the complete first sentence;
- 88.75% contain a frozen safe-routing onset marker;
- normalized decoded 16-token prefixes are unique in 80/80 rows.

The structural gate requires at least `0.95`, `0.85`, and `0.99` respectively.
These checks establish that the intervention is not merely an identical generic
prefix across rows. They do not substitute for generated safety or quality.

## 6. P0 Routes

P0 creates no optimizer or scheduler, writes no checkpoint, and restores the
exact fresh LoRA state. It uses neither proxy-dev, Core100, heldout, candidate
generation, nor a reviewer.

- malformed inputs, nonfinite or missing gradient records, budget mismatch,
  insufficient VRAM, or state drift: `STOP_RAMP_P0_INVALID`;
- structurally generic or incomplete prefixes:
  `STOP_RAMP_P0_PREFIX_NOT_BEHAVIORAL`;
- prefix and suffix gradients are not distinct:
  `STOP_RAMP_P0_PLACEBO_NOT_DISTINCT`;
- prefix direction does not replicate or beat suffix on prompt-disjoint
  validation: `STOP_RAMP_P0_DIRECTION_NOT_REPLICATED`;
- all gates pass: `PASS_RAMP_P0_AUTHORIZE_MATCHED_SMOKE_PROTOCOL`.

No threshold may change after the protocol commit.

## 7. Conditional Matched Experiment

Only a P0 pass may request a separate pushed protocol for three fresh seed-1
arms: uniform CE, RAMP, and suffix placebo. Each arm must use identical risk
order, target order, safety exposure `0.20`, optimizer schedule, number of
margin terms, and initial LoRA bytes. The frozen P0 coefficients are reused.

The first training stage is a two-step integrity smoke, followed by one
prospectively frozen 16-step development run. Free generation must beat both
uniform and suffix placebo on a non-weak paired gate. Training loss, certificate
coverage on training prompts, or gradient geometry cannot promote the method.
Blind review remains after deterministic development behavior.

## 8. Literature Boundary

RAMP's margin term is related to multiclass margin losses; Welleck et al.'s
token- and sequence-level unlikelihood training (2019, arXiv:1908.04319);
SLiC-HF sequence-likelihood calibration (Zhao et al., 2023,
arXiv:2305.10425); DPO (Rafailov et al., 2023); and TDPO's token-level
preference formulation (Zeng et al., 2024, arXiv:2404.11999). The candidate
contribution is narrower:
connect the greedy reachability certificate to prefix-only safety replay, match
its first-step auxiliary gradient budget to a same-target suffix placebo, and
require prompt-disjoint directional replication before training.

This positioning is provisional. Discovery search is not a novelty proof; a
paper claim requires full-method inspection and forward/backward citation
review. RAMP must not be described as novel or effective before that review and
the registered behavioral evidence.

Throughout P0, `optimizer_steps=0`, `training_authorized=false`,
`core100_opened=false`, and `heldout_opened=false`.
