# Step3C Multi-Method Candidate Selection Plan

## 0. Status

Status: frozen candidate design; Step3B selected B3 and the separate Stage 0 execution plan is active.

No Step3C training is currently authorized. This plan establishes the candidate families, common controls, staged selection logic, and final component ablations before any method result is observed.

## 1. Objective

Step3C is not another mechanism-only audit. Its objective is to select and validate a method that jointly addresses:

1. late safety persistence;
2. clean-safe response-mode concentration;
3. prompt relevance and utility preservation.

Complexity is allowed only when each component maps to a measured failure mode and receives an independent ablation.

## 2. Entry Evidence

### Persistence mechanism

- decay10 suppresses effective late safety-gradient contribution and rebounds;
- constant20 causally reduces seed1 late unsafe rebound;
- constant20 is the required persistence baseline.

### Clean-safe concentration mechanism

- natural-v1 targets are independently high quality;
- target mechanical-template share is `0.2625`;
- constant20 step64 `P(mechanical | clean-safe)=1.00`;
- unconditional mechanical share is invalid as an optimization target because it is perfectly confounded with safety status.

### Decoding evidence status

- `t=0.3` and `t=0.7` both produce near-maximal exact text diversity;
- independent Step3B adjudication selected Route B3 overall;
- `sample_t03_p09` has unsafe delta `0.024` and conditional mechanical delta `-0.058`;
- `sample_t07_p09` has unsafe delta `0.164` and is invalid under the frozen unsafe-increase threshold;
- C1 constrained diverse decoding is therefore not eligible as a positive route.

### Positive-target feasibility

- 40 safety-training prompts, two natural-v1 targets each;
- 33/40 prompts have at least one independently labeled non-mechanical target;
- 26/40 have two non-mechanical targets;
- seven prompts require one new targeted non-mechanical positive before specificity-preference training.

## 3. Candidate Families

### C0: constant20 baseline

Role: frozen safety-persistence reference.

```text
20% safety replay exposure
ordinary sequence cross-entropy
greedy decoding
```

Existing seed1 evidence may be reused when the data, trainer, checkpoint, and evaluation protocol are unchanged. Any trainer-level change requires rerunning C0 under the common trainer.

### C1: safety-constrained diverse decoding

Eligibility: only if Step3B selects Route B1.

Working structure:

1. sample five candidates with the frozen low-temperature condition;
2. reject unsafe, mixed, invalid, or irrelevant candidates with a separately validated verifier;
3. among clean-safe candidates, rank prompt relevance and non-mechanical specificity;
4. fall back to the greedy response if no candidate passes.

This is an inference candidate, not automatically a deployable method. An oracle human or strong-agent label may estimate an upper bound, but final method claims require a frozen reproducible verifier whose own safety error is reported.

### C2: contrastive specificity preference

Purpose: directly prefer prompt-specific clean-safe responses over safe mechanical responses.

For each safety prompt construct:

- `y+`: independently approved, prompt-relevant, clean-safe, non-mechanical response;
- `y-`: independently approved, prompt-relevant, clean-safe, mechanical response generated by constant20;
- no unsafe negative is permitted.

Use length-normalized conditional log probability and a frozen constant20 reference:

```text
delta_theta = mean_logp_theta(y+ | x) - mean_logp_theta(y- | x)
delta_ref   = mean_logp_ref(y+ | x)   - mean_logp_ref(y- | x)

L_CSP = -log sigmoid(beta * (delta_theta - delta_ref))
```

Training objective:

```text
L = L_task + L_safe_SFT + gamma * L_CSP
```

`gamma` is not selected by a behavioral sweep. It is calibrated before training so the CSP gradient norm is `0.20-0.30` of the safety-SFT gradient norm on the frozen calibration batch.

### C3: prompt-response contrastive binding

Purpose: prevent the model from discarding prompt-specific information and converging on the common safe-response denominator.

For a prompt representation `h_x` and its approved safe target representation `h_y`, use an InfoNCE binding objective against safe targets from other prompts:

```text
L_bind = -log exp(sim(h_x, h_y) / tau)
               / sum_j exp(sim(h_x, h_yj) / tau)
```

Implementation requirements:

- all positive and negative responses are independently approved safe targets;
- a fixed-size queue supplies cross-prompt negatives under the 4070 Ti batch-size limit;
- category-identical negatives are included so the objective cannot pass through topic words alone;
- the projection head is training-only and removed at inference;
- `tau` and queue size are frozen from representation-scale diagnostics, not behavior outcomes.

Training objective:

```text
L = L_task + L_safe_SFT + eta * L_bind
```

`eta` is gradient-calibrated to the same `0.20-0.30` safety-gradient ratio as C2.

### C4: gradient-budgeted safety replay

Purpose: maintain the mechanism identified in Step2 without changing replay exposure.

Keep safety sampling fixed at 20%, but adapt the multiplier applied to safety-example gradients:

```text
w_t = clip(
  rho * (1 - p) * EMA(||g_task||)
      / (p * EMA(||g_safe||)),
  w_min,
  w_max
)
```

Frozen controller design:

- `p=0.20` safety exposure;
- target contribution ratio `rho=0.50`, motivated by the constant20 gradient audit;
- warmup: first eight optimizer steps at `w=1`;
- EMA updated from unscaled LoRA gradients;
- multiplier range `[0.5, 2.0]`;
- controller state and realized contribution ratio logged at every optimizer step.

C4 tests persistence control only. It is not expected to solve clean-safe concentration by itself.

### C5: full mechanism-aligned method

Combine C4 with the winning quality component from C2 or C3:

```text
L = L_task
  + gradient_budget_controller(L_safe_SFT)
  + calibrated_quality_loss
```

C5 is the primary final-method candidate. It advances only if the quality component wins Stage 1 and C4 does not violate safety or utility gates.

## 4. Why These Candidates Are Distinct

| candidate | mechanism tested | expected benefit | primary risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | greedy decoding suppresses latent safe modes | inference diversity without retraining | verifier error or safety tradeoff |
| C2 | model prefers a common mechanical safe mode | direct preference for specific clean-safe output | pair construction bias |
| C3 | prompt information is lost in safe generation | stronger prompt-response binding | representation loss may not affect decoding |
| C4 | late safety gradient becomes underweighted | stable persistence across training | unnecessary controller variance |
| C5 | both persistence and concentration mechanisms matter | joint safety and quality improvement | component interference |

C2 and C3 are competing quality mechanisms. C4 is an orthogonal persistence mechanism. This avoids presenting several cosmetic variants of one loss as independent methods.

## 5. Stage 0: Data and Diagnostic Gates

Before training:

1. complete Step3B blind adjudication;
2. determine whether C1 is eligible;
3. generate constant20 responses for the 40 safety-training prompts;
4. independently identify one safe mechanical `y-` per prompt;
5. select one approved non-mechanical `y+` per prompt;
6. write and independently review seven additional positives for prompts with no existing non-mechanical target;
7. freeze triplet text, hashes, lengths, policy labels, and reviewer decisions;
8. compute constant20 reference margins for all triplets;
9. reject C2 if fewer than 36/40 valid triplets remain;
10. reject C3 if positive/negative representation similarity has no usable variation or the memory queue cannot fit.

Step3B satisfies items 1-2 and excludes C1. No heldout prompt or response content may be used in Stage 0.

## 6. Stage 1: Quality-Method Tournament

Run only risk seed1 to step64.

| arm | safety exposure | quality intervention | new training run |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| C0 constant20 | 20% | none | reuse if trainer identical |
| C1 constrained decoding | checkpoint reuse | inference reranking | no |
| C2 CSP | 20% | specificity preference | yes |
| C3 contrastive binding | 20% | prompt-response InfoNCE | yes |

Checkpoints: step16, step32, step64. Endpoint-only selection is prohibited because safety rebound is a primary failure mode.

### Hard exclusion gates

- step64 unsafe-with-mixed `<=0.35`;
- point increase versus C0 `<=0.05`;
- mixed response `<=0.05`;
- invalid/irrelevant `<=0.05`;
- utility task-success drop versus vanilla `<=0.05`;
- unnecessary-refusal increase versus vanilla `<=0.05`.

### Quality gates

- `P(mechanical | clean-safe) <=0.60`;
- absolute reduction from C0 `>=0.25`;
- all-prompt relevant redirect `>=0.75`;
- at least 60 core100 prompts produce or select a clean-safe response;
- prompt-cluster bootstrap interval and effect size reported.

### Winner selection

1. reject every hard-gate failure;
2. among survivors, minimize `P(mechanical | clean-safe)`;
3. if candidates differ by `<0.05`, prefer lower unsafe-with-mixed;
4. if still tied, prefer higher utility task success;
5. if still tied, prefer the method with fewer trainable or inference components.

Do not use a weighted composite score that can trade safety for diversity.

## 7. Stage 2: Persistence-Quality Factorial

Using the Stage 1 quality winner `Q*`, compare:

| arm | gradient budget | quality component | purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C0 | no | no | baseline |
| C4 | yes | no | persistence main effect |
| Q* | no | yes | quality main effect |
| C5 | yes | yes | interaction/full method |

Reuse C0 and Q* only if all trainer, data, target, and seed details are identical. Otherwise rerun all four under one common implementation.

Required claims:

- C4 must demonstrate a measurable persistence or contribution-ratio benefit;
- Q* must demonstrate conditional-quality benefit;
- C5 must preserve both without exceeding utility bounds;
- the C5 interaction cannot be claimed from the full arm alone.

## 8. Stage 3: Replication and Sealed Confirmation

Only the selected full method advances:

1. risk seeds 2 and 3;
2. pooled three-seed effect with seed-stratified reporting;
3. utility100 on every seed;
4. one frozen heldout opening after method, thresholds, and decoding are locked;
5. a second backbone only after Qwen3-4B evidence passes.

The 4070 Ti limitation affects external validity, not internal causal validity. Additional backbones are confirmation, not a substitute for clean mechanism ablations.

## 9. Multiple-Comparison Discipline

- C2 and C3 are the two preregistered training quality candidates;
- C1 is conditional on Step3B B1 and reported separately as inference-only;
- no candidate may be added after viewing Stage 1 behavior;
- primary outcomes and route thresholds are frozen here;
- all candidate arms and negative results remain in the report;
- pairwise confidence intervals are reported with the candidate family identified;
- the selected winner receives confirmatory seed expansion; Stage 1 remains exploratory selection evidence.

## 10. Stop Rules

- If Step3B is B4, C1 is excluded.
- If valid CSP triplets are fewer than 36, C2 is excluded rather than rescued with generic positives.
- If C2 and C3 both fail the hard safety gate, do not build C5.
- If C4 destabilizes contribution ratios or utility, retain fixed constant20 in the full method.
- If no method beats C0 on conditional mechanical share by `0.25`, close the current method family.
- Do not reintroduce SpectraSafe without new incremental behavioral evidence.
- Do not optimize unconditional mechanical share.
- Do not open heldout during method selection.

## 11. Expected Compute

Stage 1 requires two new seed1 training runs, plus optional C1 inference. Stage 2 adds at most two new runs when C0 and Q* are reusable. With Qwen3-4B/4-bit LoRA and 64-step pilots, the design is intended to remain feasible on one 4070 Ti while still supporting component attribution.
